# Prompt Comparison

| Technique        | Advantages                        | Result    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- | --------- |
| Zero-shot        | Quick identification of bugs      | Good      |
| Chain of Thought | Detailed explanation of every bug | Very Good |
| RTF              | Best organized response           | Excellent |

## Conclusion

Zero-shot was useful for discovering the bugs quickly.

Chain of Thought provided detailed reasoning.

RTF produced the clearest and most professional output.
